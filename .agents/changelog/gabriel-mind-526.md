Agents now know the workspace can be used from a phone. AGENTS.md says the user may be reaching the workspace from the Imbue Studio desktop app or through a sharing URL in any browser, desktop or mobile, and that the agent must never say the workspace cannot be used from a phone. A new `use-from-phone` skill walks the user through turning on sharing, getting the link onto the phone (the Share panel's "Email me a Mobile Access Link" button, or a copied link), and adding the workspace or an app to the home screen on iPhone/iPad and Android.
