Apps can now group their model calls by task. `with usage_turn(turn_id):` (in `workspace_usage.turn`) gives every LiteLLM call and `claude -p` one-shot recorded inside it that `attribution.turn_id`, and a single LiteLLM call can name its own with `metadata={"imbue_turn_id": ...}`. The LiteLLM callback reads the turn before the request is sent, so a call LiteLLM reports from a thread of its own keeps it. `build_model_call` and `record_claude_one_shot` take a `turn_id`. It is optional: an app that sets none records its calls as before.
