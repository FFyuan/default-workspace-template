`system/libs/workspace_ui/src/embed.ts` exports `HIGHLIGHT_CHROME` (`minds:highlight-chrome`, embed contract v9): the workspace asks the Imbue Studio chrome to ring one of its own controls (`workspace-switcher`, `permissions`, `settings`, `share`, `help`) while the desktop's tour talks about it, or to clear the ring with `null`. Probed off the vendored contract snapshot with a literal fallback, as the other newer constants are, so it works before the mngr pin moves.

The desktop interface contracts (`docs/system/blueprint/desktop-interface/contracts.md`, section 12) list the tour's markers: `data-tour-card`, `data-tour-badge`, `data-tour-paused`, `data-tour-exit`, `data-tour-back`, `data-tour-next`, `data-tour-link`, and the launcher's `data-tour-row`.
